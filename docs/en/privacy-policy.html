Privacy policy — Rumi

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · Developer: Sotico91

Rumi (“the App”) is a personal finance app. This policy explains what data is handled and how. By using the App you accept these practices. The Spanish version is the original; if the two differ, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Summary

Your transactions, accounts, budgets and settings are stored on your device and never leave it. Rumi has no server, does not ask you to create an account, and does not sell or share your data. Questions you ask in “Ask Rumi” are answered on the phone itself. The only thing that goes over the internet is what Rumi Pro needs (section 6), with an anonymous identifier.

2. Data the App processes

  • The name you enter in the App.
  • Transactions (amounts, concepts, notes, payment methods).
  • Accounts, debts, budgets, reminders and preferences.
  • Backups you export or restore (JSON/CSV) through the device's file system or share sheet.

3. Device permissions

  • Notifications — reminders and alerts when you log a transaction (optional).
  • Face ID / fingerprint or passcode — only if you turn on the App lock; the system does the check and Rumi never receives your fingerprint or face.
  • Files / sharing — only when you export or restore a backup.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — only if you tap the microphone to dictate an expense. Rumi listens while you speak and stops when you finish; it does not record or store the audio. If your phone has the language installed, recognition runs on the device. If not, the system (Apple or Google) turns the speech into text with its own service and under its own policy; Rumi only receives the text and reads it on the phone.

The App does not request the camera, contacts or location.

4. Notifications on the lock screen

Alerts when you log a transaction are off by default. If you turn them on, they show the amount and the category, and can be seen while the phone is locked; the eye that hides amounts inside the App does not apply to those notifications. Turn them on and off in Settings (your initial on Home) or in the phone's notification settings.

5. Phone backups

If system backups are on (iCloud on iPhone or Google backup on Android), the system may include Rumi's data in that backup, always encrypted. On iPhone, an encrypted backup also keeps the key and restores both on the new phone. On Android the key never leaves the phone: to move your data to another device use “Export backup” in Settings.

6. Third-party services

Rumi Pro. Google Play or the App Store charges the subscription, and Rumi uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to check it. When the App opens, RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier created by the App, the IP address, the phone model, the system version and, if you buy, the purchase details (plan, price, date and store country), to show the plans and know whether you have Pro. It does not receive your name, your email or your transactions.

The App includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Notifications are local and scheduled on the device. “Report a problem” opens your email app and only sends what you write. The stores (Apple / Google) may process installation data under their own policies.

7. Children

Rumi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. “Delete all data” (in Settings) removes transactions, balances, debts and limits; your categories and your name are kept. Uninstalling removes everything stored on the device; system backups (section 5) are managed from your Apple or Google account.

Asking us to delete your Rumi Pro data. The only Rumi data that leaves the phone is what RevenueCat receives (section 6): the anonymous id and, if you bought, the purchase details. To have Sotico91, Rumi's developer, delete it:

  1. In Rumi, open Settings → Rumi Pro and, at the bottom, press and hold the “Support ID” to copy it.
  2. Email edavidvelascop@gmail.com (or use Settings → Report a problem) with the subject “Delete my Rumi data” and paste that ID. If you can't open the App, tell us which store you installed it from and roughly when you bought.

Within 30 days we delete the id, IP address, device details and purchase history linked to it in RevenueCat, and confirm by email. Deleting this data does not cancel an active subscription: cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store. Google and Apple keep their own payment records under their policies. Your transactions never leave the phone; they are deleted with “Delete all data” or by uninstalling.

9. Security

Your transactions, accounts, debts, limits, categories and name are stored encrypted (AES-256) with a key that lives in the phone's secure store (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), separate from the data. We recommend turning on the App lock (Face ID / passcode) on shared devices. Backups you export are under your control and are not encrypted; protect them as sensitive information.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: edavidvelascop@gmail.com
