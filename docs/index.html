In English

Rumi is a personal finance app: log a spend in seconds, set monthly limits, get reminders for bills and loan payments, and ask in your own words how much you spent. Works in US dollars and Colombian pesos. No account, no bank connection, no server: your data stays encrypted on your phone. Android is in closed testing on Google Play (join the test, page in Spanish); iPhone is coming soon.
